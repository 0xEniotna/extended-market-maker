## 3. Pre-registered success criteria

The iter is a **success** if **all** of the following hold over the test window (≥3 weeks OR ≥100 closed trades):

1. **Primary metric — cumulative PnL**:
   - `trade_pnl + funding_fees - close_fees > 0` after fees.
   - Equivalent: ≥ $0/day average — even modest profit is fine; we're testing viability.

2. **Secondary metric — fill rate**:
   - ≥ 1 fill per active hour during NASDAQ-active periods (~13:30-20:00 UTC, ~6.5h × 5 days = 32.5h/week).
   - Target: ≥ 30 fills per week.

3. **Secondary metric — toxic-fill fraction**:
   - Mean +5s post-fill markout ≥ −2 bps (per Stage 2 markout convention, MM-perspective).
   - If <−2 bps, market may be too adverse-selecting for our spread band.

4. **Safety metric**:
   - No `drawdown_stop` triggered.
   - No `circuit_breaker` open repeatedly.
   - Quote latency p95 ≤ 1.5× baseline.

## 5. Rollback trigger (immediate stop)

The iter is **aborted and rolled back** within the test window if **any**:

- `drawdown_stop` fires (abs $150 threshold)
- Cumulative realized PnL < −$200 (10% of position cap)
- One-sided fill bias (BUY/SELL ratio < 0.3 or > 3.0) sustained for ≥ 24h during active hours
- Quote latency p95 > 2× baseline

Specifically here (BABA lesson):
- Zero fills in 48h of active NASDAQ hours → indicates quotes too wide; pause and re-evaluate MULT.
- Off-hours-only fills with adverse markout → indicates we're getting picked off by overnight informed flow; widen MIN_OFFSET.

Rollback procedure:
```bash
ssh mm-bot 'cd /root/MM && PATH=/root/MM/.venv/bin:$PATH mmctl stop goog_24_5.iter001'
```
