| HOOD | **20.5 bps** | **0.7** | TBD — wider, scale down |
| BABA | 24.5 bps | 1.0 | **FAILED** (0 fills in 17h) |

ORCL is closer to MU than to BABA, so we keep MULT=1.0. If first 8h
shows 0 fills, we know to lower MULT for the next iter.
