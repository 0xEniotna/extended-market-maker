## 3. Why MM_SPREAD_MULTIPLIER=0.7 specifically

From the BABA post-mortem (24.5 bps spread, MULT=1.0):
- Base offset = 24.5 bps each side
- After inventory skew + safety adjustments → **46 bps each side**
- Bot bid-ask spread = 92 bps vs market 24.5 = **4× too wide → 0 fills in 17h**

For HOOD with spread 20.5 bps:
- MULT=1.0 would give base 20.5 → likely 30-40 bps at fill → similar BABA-fate
- MULT=0.7 gives base 14.3 → likely 18-25 bps at fill → competitive bid/ask vs 20.5 market

The general rule from this session:
- spread ≤15 bps: MULT=1.0 OK (DOT 12bp, MU 14bp both profitable at MULT=1.0)
- spread 15-25 bps: MULT=0.6-0.8 needed (HOOD, ORCL territory)
- spread >25 bps: MULT=0.4-0.6 (would have been BABA)
